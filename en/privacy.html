PRIVACY NOTICE

How personal data is handled

Data controller: Emanuele Guerrini. For personal-data enquiries, use the website contact form. Before commercial publication, add a direct privacy contact channel and verify the legal identification details required for your activity.

Data and purposes

The contact form requests name, company, email, project country and enquiry details. These are used exclusively to review the enquiry and respond to the sender. No automated newsletter or direct-marketing activity is included.

Legal basis and retention

Processing responds to pre-contractual steps requested by the individual (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and, as relevant, explicit consent given via the form. Personal data is kept only as long as required to handle enquiries and applicable legal duties, with periodic deletion when no longer necessary.

External services

The site may load resources from Google Fonts and illustrative images from Unsplash; these providers may receive technical connection data, including IP addresses. The hosting service processes technical logs according to its terms. This project does not install profiling cookies or analytics tracking.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. Enquiries can be made through the contact form.
